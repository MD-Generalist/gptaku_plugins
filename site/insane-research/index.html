To be fair

If you just need a quick overview, ChatGPT or Gemini deep research is easier. insane-research asks a few scoping questions and takes longer. Use it for decisions where being wrong is expensive: technology choices, implementation evidence, numbers.

For decisions
you can't afford to get wrong.
